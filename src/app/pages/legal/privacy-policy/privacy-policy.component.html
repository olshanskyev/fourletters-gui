4letters ("the App", available at 4letters.app) is an end-to-end encrypted messaging application operated by Evgenii Olshanskii ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we process when you use the App and why. We are committed to data minimisation: we collect only what is required to operate a secure messenger.

1. Information we process

When you sign in and use the App, we process the following on our servers:

  • Account identity — your display name, avatar URL, and the identifier supplied by your chosen sign-in provider (Google or VK). We never receive or store your provider password.
  • Public cryptographic keys — your identity key, signed prekey, and one-time prekeys. These are public by design and are used by your contacts to establish end-to-end encrypted sessions with you.
  • Encrypted messages (temporary) — when a recipient is offline, the message is held in an inbox until it is delivered. Message content is end-to-end encrypted; we cannot read it. Delivered messages are removed from the server.
  • Group metadata — group membership, group name and avatar, required to route group messages to the correct participants.
  • Web Push subscriptions — if you enable notifications, we store the push endpoint provided by your browser so we can wake the App when a new message arrives. Notifications do not contain message content.

2. Google user data

When you choose "Sign in with Google", Google shares your basic profile information (name, profile picture) and a stable account identifier with us, solely to create and authenticate your account. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties.

3. End-to-end encryption

Message content is encrypted on your device and can only be decrypted by the intended recipient(s). Private keys never leave your device. Because of this, we have no ability to access, read, or hand over the content of your messages.

4. How we use information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your account.
  • To deliver messages between you and your contacts.
  • To send push notifications you have opted into.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

5. Data retention

  • Undelivered messages are stored only until delivery, then removed.
  • Account identity, public keys and group metadata are retained while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, associated server-side data is deleted. Data already stored end-to-end encrypted on other users' devices is outside our control.

6. Data stored on your device

The App stores your keys, decrypted conversations and settings locally in your browser (IndexedDB). Clearing your browser storage or uninstalling the App removes this local data.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the infrastructure providers required to run the service and with your chosen authentication provider (Google or VK) for sign-in.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights over your personal data, including access, rectification, erasure, and portability. To exercise these rights, contact us at olshanskyev@gmail.com.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under the age of 16.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
