4letters

A free, open-source, end-to-end encrypted messenger

4letters (available at 4letters.app) is a free, open-source messaging application. It lets you chat privately with your contacts, one-to-one or in groups, with end-to-end encryption so that only you and the people you message can read your conversations. 4letters installs directly from your browser as a Progressive Web App - there is no app store and no tracking.

What you can do

  • Send end-to-end encrypted one-to-one and group messages.
  • Share images securely with your contacts.
  • Receive push notifications when new messages arrive.
  • Install the app to your device straight from the browser.

Signing in with Google

To create your account, you can sign in with Google. When you do, we use Google Sign-In only to verify your identity and to set up your profile. Specifically, we request:

  • Your basic profile (name and profile picture) - used to display your account and let your contacts recognise you.
  • Your email address - used solely as a unique identifier for your account. We do not send marketing email.

We never gain access to your Google password, contacts, or any other Google data, and we never read the content of your messages. Your message content is end-to-end encrypted and cannot be read by our servers.

Your privacy

We are committed to data minimisation and collect only what is required to operate a secure messenger. For full details of what we process and why, please read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
